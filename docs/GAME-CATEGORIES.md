Updated 22 September 2026. The running pack now contains **10 categories, 50 slots, 42 playable clues and 8 clearly marked drafts**, in English and Norwegian Bokmål. The host chooses six columns for a 30-question board. The default six are complete, so a rehearsal has no blocked tiles.

## The birthday board once the personal material is ready

Use **Famous Saras · The Birthday Girl · What’s That Country? · The Roaring Twenties · The Sara Archives · Name That Tune**. Swap music for Bad Movie Plots if that suits the guests better. Hold My Drink and The Impostor are ready substitutes when you want less celebrity content.

### Four missing face mashups

The fixed instruction is: “Sara has taken over. Who is the original celebrity?” These are proposed targets, not finished assets or claims about the existing memes:

| Slot | Proposed target | Composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 200 | Gordon Ramsay | Recognisable kitchen and chef styling; no visible name. |
| 300 | Taylor Swift | Recognisable stage styling, without a tour logo giving the answer away. |
| 400 | Rihanna | Distinctive styling with fewer background clues. |
| 500 | Ryan Gosling | A closer blend with fewer costume clues; reorder after rehearsal. |

Each needs a suitable original reference, Sara’s face reference, a completed blend and the exact accepted celebrity name. A supplied reaction sticker does not establish an underlying celebrity. Keep missing images as drafts, and do not use an invented answer. Original-versus-mashup comparison images can be prepared later; the existing clue renderer does not yet show a comparison pair.

### Four missing Sara stories

- **200:** One travel photo with its confirmed city or country. Ask something guests can infer from the image.
- **300:** A real Sara quote, with context and plausible choices; confirm who said it.
- **400:** A short submitted story, stopped just before the ending. The host confirms what really happened.
- **500:** A more difficult memory or matching puzzle, with enough context for newer friends to participate.

Keep these in a private host pack. Guest contributions should not be copied automatically into the public repository. Ask only about material the host has reviewed and is happy to show at the party.

## Reactions

**Correct:** the pink-sweater pose (`reaction-02.webp`). **Wrong:** the sceptical face (`reaction-06.webp`). Only these two are selected. The game animates their entrance; the files themselves are static. Category selection does not change the reaction pair.

## Rehearsal checklist

- Check all six selected columns and map silhouettes on the actual television.
- Play a wrong → another team → correct → undo sequence.
- Try both languages; accept sensible pronunciations, spellings and translated film titles.
- Keep the timer generous for picture clues and the two-part geography question.
- Try a complete board and reload mid-clue. Drafts remain unplayable and do not count towards completion.
- Treat this public bank as rehearsal material: the source code includes its answers.
