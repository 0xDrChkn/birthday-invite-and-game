FOR THE HOST

How to play

  1. Connect your laptop to the TV. Make two to six teams and enter their names.
  2. A team chooses a category and points. Click the clue, select the answering team, and start the optional timer.
  3. Award or deduct the clue value. After a wrong answer, another team can try. Reveal the answer when you’re ready.
  4. Finish the clue to mark it played. Undo fixes a scoring mistake, even after returning to the board.

“To prepare” tiles need personal questions or pictures. They won’t open until that content is added.
